# Decathlon Retail Performance Analysis (Power BI Dashboard)
A comprehensive business intelligence report evaluating sales performance, profitability, order logistics, return patterns, and strategic growth opportunities across retail outlets.

## 📌 Executive Overview
- **Period Covered:** Year-To-Date (YTD) through 15th September 2026 (benchmarked against PYTD 2025).   
- **Footprint:** 30 retail store locations spanning 5 distinct geographical operating regions.   
- **Core Objective:** Monitor top-line sales momentum, protect gross margin health, diagnose operational friction in returns, and surface data-driven commercial recommendations.   


## 🔍 In-Depth Analytical Findings

### Revenue:
- The Hiking category represents the largest revenue share at ₹27M (over 48% of total net sales), significantly outperforming the category average benchmark of ₹12.10M.
-  **Balanced Secondary Core:** Secondary sports categories maintain stable revenue distribution:
    Badminton (Perfly): ₹14M
    Cricket (Sport Practice): ₹13M
    Fitness (Domyos): ₹13M
    Cycling (Rockrider): ₹12M

- **Product Margin Distribution:**
A significant proportion of SKUs yield margins between 38% and 45%, but the majority generate under ₹1.5M in sales volume, indicating opportunities to scale selective high-margin merchandise.

### Geographic & Regional Spread:
- **Sales Strongholds:** The South (₹17.51M / ~31.7%) and West (₹14.94M / ~27.0%) regions contribute ~58.7% of aggregate business. North follows closely at ₹12.40M.   
- **Expansion Territories:** East (₹7.70M) and Central (₹2.71M) remain heavily under-penetrated, presenting prime retail footprint opportunities.
  
### Order Returns & Reverse Logistics:
- **Return Rate Spike:** Net return volume grew +26.14% YoY, surpassing the total order growth rate (+23.0%).
- **Primary Return Drivers:**
    -- **Changed Mind:** 1,139 returns (~33.3%)
    -- **Size or Fit:** 1,119 returns (~32.7%)
    -- **Not As Expected:** 827 returns (~24.2%)
    -- **Quality Issues:** 339 returns (~9.9%)
- **High-Friction SKUs:** High-turnover small accessories and basic apparel generate the highest return volumes, led by Swimming Caps (176 units), Running Socks (166 units), and Resistance Bands (161 units).
- **Store Discrepancies:** Highest relative return rates were recorded at Jaipur Sports Store (4.25%) and Panaji Sports Store (4.04%).


## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations
### Mitigate Sizing Friction:
  - Introduce standardized digital fit finders, comprehensive dimensional charts, and in-store fitting checks for high-return categories (swim caps, running socks, fitness wear) to reduce the ~65% return volume driven by fit and expectation mismatch.
  - **Basket Building & Cross-Merchandising:** With AOV growth sluggish (+1.1%), deploy complementary bundle kits (e.g., Quechua daypacks + water bottles, or badminton rackets + grip tapes/shuttlecock tubes) to push average ticket sizes past ₹2,500.
  - **Store Logistics Audit:** Conduct reverse-logistics and product quality audits at high-return outlets (Jaipur and Panaji) to identify inventory handling issues or regional fulfillment defects.
